FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Saaf free?

Yes. Saaf is free with ads. If you would rather have no ads, a one-time purchase removes them for good, and it stays with your Google Play account if you move to a new phone.

Will it delete anything without asking?

Never. Swiping only marks items. You confirm once before they go to the bin, and nothing is deleted for good until you empty it.

What if I bin something by mistake?

Open the bin and restore it. Photos, videos and audio stay in Android's trash for up to 30 days; documents stay in Saaf's bin until you delete them.

Does Saaf upload my photos?

No. There is no Saaf server and no account. Previews and similarity checks are made on your phone and deleted when you uninstall the app.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 11 and newer. For the best results, choose "Allow all" when Android asks about photo access, so Saaf can see every folder.

Why does Saaf ask about contacts or usage access?

Only for the features that need them, and only when you open those screens. Contacts are used to find duplicates; usage access shows when you last opened each app. Both are optional.
